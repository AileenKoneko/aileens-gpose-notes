Aileen's Gpose Notes

This site is a collection of notes from experimenting with ReShade and gpose in Final Fantasy XIV.

Each effect page explains what an effect tries to recreate, what visual or artistic purpose it can serve, and how different settings change the final image. The goal isn’t to prescribe a “correct” look, but to make the effects easier to understand and intentionally use.

Most examples and setting breakdowns are based on the iMMERSE stack from Marty’s Mods. I prefer it because many of its controls mirror concepts from real cameras and lenses I’m already familiar with, making experimentation feel more intuitive and deliberate.

Wherever possible, I also list alternatives — including older, free, or built-in effects — because good screenshots come from intentional choices, not expensive shader stacks.
