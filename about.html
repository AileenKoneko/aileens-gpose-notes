Aileen Koneko

I’m Aileen, an overcaffeinated engineering goblin who got way too interested in why some gposes feel like screenshots and others feel like photographs.

I tend to approach visual effects the same way I approach technical systems: strip them down to primitives, figure out what they’re actually doing, then intentionally abuse them for art.

These notes started as a way to document what I was learning about ReShade, lighting, bloom, depth of field, and cinematic rendering in FFXIV. The goal isn’t to prescribe a “correct” look — just to explain what different effects do and how they shape the feeling of an image.

My own style leans toward photographic and cinematic lighting, realistic light behavior, noir-ish contrast, and effects that help scenes feel captured rather than rendered. Yours doesn’t have to.

If this guide helps you build something weird, beautiful, dreamy, dramatic, or entirely unreadable in the pursuit of art, then it did its job.
